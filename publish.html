The Journal

What We Publish

ISE's journal is an ISSN-certified publication dedicated to original research by high school students. We publish across all STEM disciplines — from biology and chemistry to engineering, computer science, mathematics, and environmental science.

Every submission goes through a structured review process led by Dr. Ellen Roche, Associate Professor of Mechanical Engineering at MIT. We evaluate clarity of question, rigor of methodology, and quality of writing — not name, school, or prior experience.

  • Biology & Life Sciences
  • Chemistry & Biochemistry
  • Physics & Astronomy
  • Computer Science & AI
  • Mathematics & Statistics
  • Engineering & Design
  • Environmental Science
  • Psychology & Neuroscience
  • Other STEM Fields
Who Can Submit

Eligibility

  • 01Currently enrolled high school student (grades 9–12)
  • 02Original, unpublished research or literature review
  • 03All STEM fields — biology, chemistry, physics, CS, math, environmental science, engineering, and more
  • 04Solo or co-authored submissions welcome
  • 05No prior publication experience required
  • 06Students outside CAMS are welcome — open to all high schools
How It Works

Submission Process

  1. 1

    Download a Template

    Choose the template below that matches your paper type and format your submission accordingly before sending it in.

  2. 2

    Email Your Submission

    Send your formatted paper to iseofficialjournal@gmail.com with the subject line "ISE Journal Submission — [Your Name]". Attach your paper as a .docx or PDF.

  3. 3

    Review

    Your paper enters our review process, led by Dr. Ellen Roche and our faculty panel. We may reach out with revision requests during this stage.

  4. 4

    Decision & Publication

    You'll receive a decision by email. Accepted papers are published in ISE's next journal cycle and formally indexed under the journal's ISSN.
