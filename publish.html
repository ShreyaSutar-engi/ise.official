The Journal

What We Publish

ISE's journal is an ISSN-certified publication dedicated to original research by high school students. We publish across all STEM disciplines — from biology and chemistry to engineering, computer science, mathematics, and environmental science.

Every submission goes through a structured review process led by Dr. Ellen Roche, Associate Professor of Mechanical Engineering at MIT, and supported by a panel of professors. We look at clarity of question, rigor of methodology, and quality of writing — not name, school, or prior experience.

Eligibility

Who Can Submit

  • Currently enrolled high school student (grades 9–12)
  • Original, unpublished research or literature review
  • All STEM fields accepted — biology, chemistry, physics, CS, math, environmental science, engineering, and more
  • Solo or co-authored submissions accepted
  • No prior publication experience required
  • Students outside CAMS are welcome to submit
How It Works

Submission Process

  1. 1

    Download a Template

    Choose the template that matches your paper type below and format your submission accordingly before sending it in.

  2. 2

    Email Your Submission

    Send your formatted paper to iseofficialjournal@gmail.com with the subject line "ISE Journal Submission — [Your Name]".

  3. 3

    Review

    Your paper will be reviewed by Dr. Roche and our faculty panel. We may reach out with revision requests or questions during this stage.

  4. 4

    Decision & Publication

    You'll receive a decision by email. Accepted papers are published in ISE's next journal cycle and indexed under the journal's ISSN.
