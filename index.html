Our Story

ISE was founded by Shreya Sutar and Ayush Kumar — two 10th graders at CAMS — to give high school students a real chance to explore their interests and conduct genuine scientific research. Not a simulation. Not a club project. A real journal, reviewed by real professors.

Est. July 2025  ·  California Academy of Mathematics and Science

ISE is an ISSN-certified publication open to any high school student with original research to share. Papers are reviewed by MIT professors and evaluated on clarity, methodology, and contribution — not school, name, or prior experience.

Built by Students, For Students

Research, Mentorship & Community

ISE exists because we believe high school students are capable of producing real, rigorous research — not just learning about it. Every member chooses a question they care about and is guided through every stage of the research process, from question to publication.
